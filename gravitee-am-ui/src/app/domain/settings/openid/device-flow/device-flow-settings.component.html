Device Flow

The OAuth 2.0 Device Authorization Grant (RFC 8628) lets a device with no browser, or with limited input capabilities such as a TV or a printer, obtain tokens. The device shows a short user code, and the end user enters it on a second device to authorize the pairing while the first device polls the token endpoint.

Applications opt in by carrying the urn:ietf:params:oauth:grant-type:device_code grant type, and may override the timings below from their own OAuth settings.
